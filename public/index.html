    Terms & Privacy

    Last updated: October 2026

    Welcome to CodeRelay, a shared coding workspace by Crown Lab Team. By using CodeRelay you agree to these terms. They are short and written in plain language.

    1. Shared workspaces

    A workspace is created with a private link and QR code. Anyone who has the link can open the workspace and, unless the owner has locked it, edit the code. Share the link only with people you want to collaborate with.

    2. Owner controls

    The workspace owner can lock editing at any time. While locked, other participants can still read and copy the code, but cannot change it. The owner can also delete the workspace, which permanently removes its saved content.

    3. Your code and data

    Your code belongs to you and your team. CodeRelay does not claim any ownership of it. We store only what is needed to run the service: the latest version of the workspace content, the display names and numbers of participants, and basic technical data needed to keep the service secure and reliable.

    4. Security

    Workspace content is encrypted at rest on our servers using AES-256-GCM, and access to a workspace requires its link. We take reasonable care to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. For this reason, we recommend keeping sensitive credentials, such as passwords and private keys, in a dedicated secrets manager rather than in shared code.

    5. Acceptable use

    Use CodeRelay for lawful learning, collaboration and development. Please do not use it to harm others, to distribute malware, to infringe someone else's rights, to attack or disrupt the service, or to attempt to access workspaces you were not invited to. Running code happens in a restricted sandbox in your browser; do not try to bypass it.

    6. Content and removal

    You are responsible for the content you add. We may remove a workspace or restrict access if it is used in breach of these terms or to protect the service and its users.

    7. Feedback

    If you send feedback, you allow us to use it to improve CodeRelay. Please do not include personal or confidential information in feedback.

    8. Availability

    We work to keep CodeRelay running smoothly, but the service is provided "as is" and may occasionally be unavailable or change. Keep a copy of important code in your own project or repository.

    9. Limitation of liability

    To the extent permitted by law, Crown Lab Team is not liable for indirect or consequential losses arising from use of the service. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under applicable law.

    10. Changes and contact
